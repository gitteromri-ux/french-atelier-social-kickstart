Taste · Paris · 1765

The Birth of the Restaurant.

Before Boulanger sold bouillon on rue des Poulies, no one "went out to eat." Restaurant was a broth, then a place, then a French export the world adopted whole.

Culture Capsule · 15 min
Music · Left Bank · 1789 to 1968

The Café and the Idea.

Diderot at the Procope, Sartre at Les Deux Magots, Beauvoir at the Flore. French thought was written at zinc counters.

III.
Culture Capsule · 15 min
Travel · Paris · 1853 to 1870

Haussmann's Paris.

Twenty thousand demolitions. Forty thousand new buildings. Every avenue, every zinc roof, every wrought-iron balcony carries a word invented for it.

IV.
Culture Capsule · 15 min
Beauty · Grasse and Rue Cambon

The Language of Perfume.

Sillage, longevity, projection, nez. The words the great houses invented for a sense English never bothered to name.

V.
Culture Capsule · 15 min

Reading Monet's Light.

The Impressionists rewrote how French sees light. Nuée, brume, lumière, chatoiement. That is the language every student inherits.

Culture Capsule · 15 min

Versailles as Grammar.

The court, the hierarchy, the vous behind every French politeness that survives today. Louis XIV built a language of deference in stone and mirror.
