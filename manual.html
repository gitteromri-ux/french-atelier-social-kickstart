§ 01 · How the platform works

One queue. One approver.
Zero manual publishing.

Sprout Social sits on top of the three connected profiles as the single publishing brain. Every post is drafted once, given a Facebook version and an Instagram version, scheduled to a specific date and time, then routed to Philippe as External Approver. Philippe sees only the posts waiting on him, approves with one click per post, and Sprout auto-publishes at the scheduled minute to the correct network. No spreadsheets, no reminders, no copy-paste into Facebook or Instagram. The same queue also feeds the reporting engine, so every published post is measured automatically the moment it goes live.

§ 02 · How posts auto-upload and go live

Approve once.
Publish for seven weeks.

All 23 calendar posts are already inside Sprout as drafts, each with its own Facebook copy and Instagram copy, its own image or video, and its own exact publish time locked to Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9:00 Israel time from September 9 through October 30. Philippe receives an email from Sprout the moment posts enter his approval queue, opens the External Approver link (no Sprout seat needed), and approves. From that moment forward, Sprout uploads the asset to Facebook and Instagram at the scheduled minute using the official Meta Graph API. Nobody logs into Meta. Nobody copies captions. Nobody uploads media manually.

§ 04 · How auto-comment replies work

A comment with buying intent
becomes a sales conversation
in under 30 seconds.

Meta Business Suite watches every comment on every French Atelier and Longevity Life Academy post. When a comment contains any of the pre-configured buying-intent keywords (price, sign me up, DM me, interested, and their French equivalents), the automation fires two things at once. First, a public reply lands under the comment thanking the person and telling them to check their inbox. Second, an instant private DM arrives in their inbox with a direct link to the sales team, a WhatsApp number, and a booking calendar. The person went from casual scroll to booked call without a human touching a keyboard.
