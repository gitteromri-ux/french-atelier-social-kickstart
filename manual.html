One human click to activate.

The Meta Comment-to-DM rule is ninety percent configured. To activate it, click the button, find the draft rule Buying Intent Auto DM · LLA, scroll to Private message, paste the DM text below, click Save changes bottom-right.

Step one · open Meta Automations

Open Meta Business Suite Automations → https://business.facebook.com/latest/inbox/automated_responses?business_id=1489173449541995&asset_id=1179505785236150 Opens directly on the LLA Facebook Page automations screen. The draft rule is already there waiting for the private-message body.

Step two · paste this exact DM text

DM body · paste into Private message field
Merci for your interest in the French Atelier. Book a call with our team: https://frenchatelierlive.com/enrollment WhatsApp direct: https://wa.me/33XXXXXXXXX Reply here and a real person from our Paris team writes back within the hour.

Step three · click Save changes

Bottom-right of the panel. Rule flips from Draft to Live. Every buying-intent comment on LLA Facebook Page now auto-DMs the copy above within seconds.
