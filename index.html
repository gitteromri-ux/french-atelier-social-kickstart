01
Where we are · and where we go

The account is quiet. From today, it speaks.

  • i.Baseline reality. 16,875 followers on Instagram, 6,700 on Facebook, silent since Bastille Day. Latent reach exists. Attention does not, yet.
  • ii.Audience. American adults, aspirational Francophiles, drawn to beauty, taste, craft, savoir-faire. They come for the culture, they buy the lessons.
  • iii.Cadence. Three posts a week, Monday, Wednesday, Friday. One pillar per day. Every post ties to a live moment in France or a signature Atelier proof.
  • iv.Format bias. Video first, ninety percent motion. Static reserved for the two most editorial email hero moments per month.
  • v.Voice. Cormorant italic, cream on ink, gold. Never marketing shout. Curated as a magazine, sold as a school.
  • vi.North-star metric. Save-to-share ratio, then landing-page CTR from bio, then trial starts. Reach follows.

The one belief we shift

Americans learn French from apps. We show them the culture apps cannot teach. When the language arrives inside the culture, the decision to enrol writes itself.

Real-time · France, right now

There is a lot of hot stuff going on in France.

The seven-week window between now and the end of October is the richest cultural runway of the year. Fashion Week Spring Summer 27, the Louvre and Orsay opening the fall shows, Fondation Louis Vuitton reopening, harvest festivals in Montmartre and Saint-Émilion, Festival Lumière in Lyon, Art Basel Paris and the Salon du Chocolat. Every post rides one of these moments.
